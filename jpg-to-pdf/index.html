Turn JPG and JPEG photos into one PDF

JPG and JPEG are common formats for phone photos, camera pictures, receipts and scanned snapshots. Pic2PDF lets you put a group of those images in a deliberate order, choose how they fit on the page, and export one document instead of sharing a stack of separate files.

FAQ

JPG to PDF questions

Can I convert JPG and JPEG files together?

Yes. JPG and JPEG are both accepted. You can also include PNG or WebP images in the same batch.

Can I put multiple JPG photos into one PDF?

Yes. Pic2PDF supports up to 60 images per batch and places each image on its own PDF page.

Will converting a JPG make the photo sharper?

No. The converter places the image in a PDF; it does not recreate missing detail in the original photo. Start with the highest-resolution source you have.

Can I control the size of each PDF page?

Yes. Choose Auto, A4, A5, Letter or Legal, then set orientation and margins. Auto page size uses the image dimensions and selected DPI.
