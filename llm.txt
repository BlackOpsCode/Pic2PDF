# Pic2PDF

> Pic2PDF is a browser-based image-to-PDF tool for converting JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP images into PDF documents. Users can arrange images, choose page sizes and DPI settings, and download the resulting PDF.

Pic2PDF focuses on a simple, image-first conversion workflow with live previews, image ordering, and configurable PDF output.

## Main tool

- [Image to PDF Converter](https://pic2pdf.runtime-hub.com/): Convert supported images into a PDF document.
- [JPG to PDF](https://pic2pdf.runtime-hub.com/jpg-to-pdf/): Convert JPG and JPEG photographs into PDF.
- [PNG to PDF](https://pic2pdf.runtime-hub.com/png-to-pdf/): Convert PNG images, screenshots, and graphics into PDF.
- [WebP to PDF](https://pic2pdf.runtime-hub.com/webp-to-pdf/): Convert WebP images into PDF documents.
- [Multiple Images to PDF](https://pic2pdf.runtime-hub.com/multiple-images-to-pdf/): Combine multiple images into one PDF.
- [High-Quality Image to PDF](https://pic2pdf.runtime-hub.com/high-quality-image-to-pdf/): Learn about DPI, image resolution, and PDF print sizing.
